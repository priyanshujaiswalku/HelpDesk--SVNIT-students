Brief Writeup


Sardar Vallabhbhai National Institute of Technology (SVNIT), Surat was established in 1961 as one of the RECs and was declared an "Institute of National Importance" by the Government of India. The Institute offers top-tier Undergraduate (B.Tech.), Postgraduate (M.Tech.), Integrated M.Sc., and Ph.D. programmes across diverse engineering and applied sciences, including cutting-edge specializations like Mathematics & Computing, Artificial Intelligence, and Advanced Computational Sciences.
